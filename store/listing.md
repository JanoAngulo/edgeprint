## Full description (4000 max)

```
Edgeprint puts your camera settings on your photos: camera model, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, date and place, in a clean frame you'd be happy to post.

Pick a photo and Edgeprint reads its details automatically. Choose a style, adjust anything you like, and save at full resolution. Your original is never changed.

25 STYLES
• Bar: a classic info strip under the photo, with your camera brand's logo
• Frame: borders, instant-photo, film 35 and 120, and gallery mats
• Poster: card, gallery and shadow layouts
• Overlay: details printed right on the image, from a strip to a signature
• Editorial: magazine and side-panel layouts with a title and quote
• Special: blur, dark panel and header

MAKE IT YOURS
• Camera brand logos for Canon, Fujifilm, Leica, Nikon, Sony, Samsung and many more, or upload your own
• A choice of fonts
• Add your name, a title or a quote
• Show or hide the logo, date, location and author
• Crop to 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 or 16:9 for any feed
• Rename camera models the way you like ("SM-S948B" becomes "Galaxy S26 Ultra")

BUILT FOR PHOTOGRAPHERS
• Saves at full resolution, up to 50 MP
• Keeps the original camera details in the saved file
• Edit many photos at once
• Share straight from your Gallery to Edgeprint

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Edgeprint doesn't request internet access, so it can't upload your photos. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Want to keep where you took a photo private? Turn off "Keep GPS in saved files" before you post.
```
